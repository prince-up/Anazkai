# Interview Perspective Rules

All responses to questions should be concise, structured, and optimized for technical interviews.

## Guidelines

1. **Keep it Concise**: Avoid long paragraphs and fluff. Use short sentences and structured lists.
2. **Interview-Focused Structure**:
   - **Core Concept**: 1-2 sentence high-level definition/answer (easy to say out loud in an interview).
   - **Key Points / Mechanics**: Bullet points explaining how it works, why it matters, or trade-offs (time/space complexity, etc.).
   - **Common Interview Questions / Tips**: Highlight common gotchas, optimizations, or variations that interviewers frequently ask.
3. **Professional & Direct Tone**: Speak clearly and technically, using precise terminology.
